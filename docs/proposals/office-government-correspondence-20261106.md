## First Government module

This is the first bounded Government workflow, not a complete government records platform. It covers an operational correspondence register for incoming and outgoing letters, memoranda, circulars, service requests, meeting actions, and other correspondence. It is designed for synthetic or explicitly non-sensitive operational metadata only.

The owner's blueprint calls for electronic correspondence, manual official references, departmental routing, approvals, records management, archives, and audit. This first workflow implements intake, manual registration, routing, one-step maker/checker review, manual dispatch logging, and append-only activity history. Archives, retention/disposition schedules, classification, document storage, multi-step configurable approval chains, official integrations, budgets, procurement, HR, and assets remain separate future modules.

## Workflow

- **Incoming:** a team member records a received item; an Office Team owner/admin registers the institution-issued reference and routes it to an internal department and active team member. The assignee starts work and requests review; a different owner/admin approves or returns it; an owner/admin closes it. Admins may re-route an item that is registered, routed, in progress, or returned for changes. Content is frozen once the official reference is registered.
- **Outgoing:** a team member creates a draft and may relate it to an incoming item; the draft can be routed internally; the author/assignee requests review; a different owner/admin approves or returns it. An owner/admin enters the institution-issued reference and records that dispatch happened, including a method and optional dispatch reference; the record can then be closed. Content is editable only before approval/registration or after a return for changes.
- **Reference numbers are never generated by COCCE.** Registration requires the human-entered reference issued under the institution's own approved process. References are unique within a workspace and direction.
- **Dispatch is only recorded, not performed.** This module does not send email, post, or transmit correspondence.
- **No hard-delete RPC exists.** Terminal records are closed or cancelled and remain auditable. Retention, archival, disposition, and any destructive rollback require a separate approved policy.

## Access and audit

- Uses the existing COCCE Office workspace, membership, and authenticated identity. It adds only `office_gov_correspondence*` objects plus private validation helpers; it does not change existing Office/COCCE tables, Auth, RLS, Realtime, wallet, or storage configuration. It requires the existing shared-industry profile table.
- Direct table access is revoked. All reads and writes go through `SECURITY DEFINER` RPCs with an empty fixed `search_path`, active-membership checks, workspace-bound foreign keys, and version checks.
- Owners/admins can view and route workspace records. Members can see records they created or that are currently assigned to them. Members may edit their own unregistered drafts/received items; only owners/admins may register, route/re-route, approve, dispatch, close, or cancel. Registered content cannot be edited by admins through the application RPCs.
- Approval requires a reviewer different from both the record creator and current assignee. This is a single reviewer gate, not a configurable public-sector approval hierarchy.
- Database checks and triggers require contiguous event versions whose destination matches the current record and whose source status matches the preceding event; they reject ordinary update/delete/truncate mutations to event rows and constrain event/status combinations. Events record actors, actions, changed field names, departments, and assignee movement, but deliberately do not copy subject/summary contents. This is not tamper-evident against a privileged database operator who can alter the schema or disable triggers.

## Data, files, and legal boundaries

- Do not enter citizen names/IDs, addresses, case narratives, classified/security information, confidential legal content, or other personal, sensitive, restricted, or regulated records. The app cannot inspect free text to guarantee that it is non-sensitive.
- The record subject and summary are metadata, not an approved document repository. The workflow stores no document bytes or attachments. Bunny storage is not called; storage credits are exhausted. No old device-local items are imported.
- Current assignees can see the subject, summary, and full event trail, including prior routing identifiers. Reassignment changes who can see the record through the app; records officers must assess this access model before real use.
- No file plan, classification scheme, retention period, archival transfer, legal hold, or disposal rule is inferred. The institution's records officer must define the applicable records series and schedule before any real-record deployment.
- No connection to Tanzania ERMS, e-GA, email, the Registry Procedures Manual system, or another official government service is claimed or implemented.
- This is not an approved government records system, legal/compliance advice, statutory reference-number service, or records-retention engine. Real government use requires institutional approval and applicable privacy/security/legal review.
